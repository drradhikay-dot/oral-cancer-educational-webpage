# Oral Cancer Educational Web Resource

An interdisciplinary oral cancer informatics project integrating clinical dentistry, health informatics, and web development.

## Project Overview

This academic project was developed to organize and communicate evidence-based information related to oral cancer through a web-based educational resource.

The project combines my background in dentistry with graduate training in Health Informatics and explores the use of digital technologies for presenting clinical information in an accessible and structured format.

## Topics Covered

- Oral cancer overview
- Etiology and risk factors
- Clinical signs and symptoms
- TNM staging
- Diagnostic approaches
- Treatment
- Scholarly references

## Technologies

- HTML
- Web Content Development
- Digital Health Information
- Healthcare Information Organization

## Skills Demonstrated

- Health Informatics

- Healthcare Information Organization

- Digital Health Content Development

This project reflects my interdisciplinary interest in oral oncology, health informatics, disease detection, and digital technologies supporting healthcare information and diagnostic research.
